ABOUT // HEMANTH

Hemanth is a game developer and systems engineer driven by a simple objective: understand the machinery behind interactive experiences. His work spans gameplay, rendering, XR, simulation, AI, networking, GPU programming and performance engineering, with an increasing focus on ECS, large-scale simulation and robotics. Rather than simply using existing systems, he builds, breaks and rebuilds them to understand how they work—from game mechanics and VR interactions to renderers, GPU-driven systems and inverse kinematics. The objective is not to master a particular tool, but to continually push deeper into the technology and build better systems along the way.
